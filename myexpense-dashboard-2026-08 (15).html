☁ Enable Cloud Sync

Cloud Sync stores your expenses in a free Firebase database. Share your tracker once, and everyone sees updates automatically in real-time. No more sending files back and forth!

💡 Setup Steps (takes 2 minutes):

  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com
  2. Click "Create a project" → name it "myexpense" → Continue → Continue → Create project
  3. In the left sidebar, click "Realtime Database" → "Create Database"
  4. Choose "Start in test mode" → click "Enable"
  5. Click the gear icon (Project Settings) at top left
  6. Under "Your apps", click the "</>" (Web) icon
  7. Give it a nickname (e.g., "tracker") → click "Register app"
  8. Copy the entire firebaseConfig object and paste below
